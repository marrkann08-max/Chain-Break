6 · Model assumptions & limits

Transparent by design

Prototype
Loading
—
  • Worst case: the track is blocked, so the following car must stop before the stationary car.
  • Braking is a conservative 1.1 g scaled by grip — below the peak deceleration of a modern single-seater.
  • Intervention responses (target speed, deceleration, VSC decision time) are stated assumptions, not measured driver behaviour.
  • Outcomes are simulated. They are not crash probabilities and this is not a certified safety system.
